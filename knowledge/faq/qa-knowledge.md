### Q6: How does VoteWise ensure secure digital voting?

### Q23: How does Sujit approach debugging complex bugs?
Sujit uses a disciplined 4-step debugging approach:
1. **Reproduce**: Create a minimal reproducible test case.
2. **Isolate**: Trace execution through logging, breakpoints, and component boundary isolation.
3. **Root Cause**: Identify the fundamental flaw rather than applying surface-level patches.
4. **Verify & Regress**: Write automated tests to confirm the fix and prevent future regressions.
