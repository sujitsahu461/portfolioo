# CivicSetu

## Problem
Citizens and urban local bodies need a clear, trackable way to report and resolve civic grievances.

## Solution
CivicSetu is a full-stack civic engagement and municipal grievance redressal platform. It digitizes public complaint reporting and gives citizens and municipal authorities role-based workflows with transparent lifecycle tracking and auditability.

## Technologies
Django, Django REST Framework, JWT authentication, MySQL, React, Vite, Zustand, Leaflet for incident maps.
